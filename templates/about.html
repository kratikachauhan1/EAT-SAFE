About EAT SAFE

Our mission is to reduce food allergen risks by transforming complex packaging fine print into instant, personalized safety insights.

Accessibility First

We believe basic food safety screening should be free and accessible to all. Guest users can scan packaging labels instantly without friction or mandatory account barriers.

Health Personalization

Every individual's dietary requirements are unique. Registered users configure tailored allergen profiles, custom terms, and barcode history for immediate protection.

Open Data & Verification

Integrating Open Food Facts API and robust local computer vision ensures reliable product lookup backed by fallback label OCR verification.

Technology Stack & Standards Compliance

Backend Framework Python 3.14 + Flask micro-framework
Computer Vision & OCR OpenCV + Tesseract OCR Engine
Regulatory Standards FSSAI Food Safety Regulations
Storage & DB Dual SQLite / PostgreSQL + S3 Storage
