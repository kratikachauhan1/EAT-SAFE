How EAT SAFE Works

Assistive Computer Vision + NLP Food Packaging Analysis Engine

1

Image Preprocessing

When you upload a package photo or scan a label using your device camera, OpenCV converts the image to grayscale, applies adaptive thresholding, reduces Gaussian noise, and sharpens text boundaries.

Grayscale, Adaptive Threshold & Contrast Equalization
2

Tesseract OCR Extraction

Tesseract OCR extracts raw textual ingredient statements from the preprocessed image frame and assigns an average character confidence score to evaluate readability.

Confidence-weighted text string extraction
3

NLP Section Tokenization

The NLP tokenizer divides text into Explicit Ingredients (e.g. "Ingredients: Wheat Flour, Milk Solids...") and Precautionary Cautions (e.g. "May contain traces of peanuts...").

Distinguishes direct ingredients vs cross-contact cautions
4

Personalized Risk Match

Extracted tokens are cross-referenced with your active allergy profile (e.g., Peanuts, Whey, Casein, Gluten) and custom terms to generate immediate HIGH RISK or CAUTION risk badges.

Exact & fuzzy derivative term matching

Assistive Screening Tool Disclaimer

EAT SAFE is designed as an assistive screening utility to highlight potential allergen derivatives on food packaging labels. It does not constitute medical advice or guarantee 100% food safety. Users with life-threatening allergies must always physically inspect product packaging prior to consumption.
